# Entry 1: choosing a topic (meteorology)
##### 10/24/26

I decide to land on the topic of meteorology for this project as its something that I've always found interesting and I want to have a career in this topic too, technology is a important part of meteorology as its used to predict weather patterns and with the advance of technology the new technology can configured or trained to study weather patterns and made predictions, with AI advancing the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA) has made an attempt to [train AI to make weather models](https://www.noaa.gov/news-release/noaa-deploys-new-generation-of-ai-driven-global-weather-models) this one of the ways new technology has been brought into meteorology, but its not the first time new technology has been created to help in weather prediction, 
Advanced Weather Interactive Processing System or AWIPS first introduced in 1998 is a system of complex software that displays and analyzes weather and hydrological information and displays it for the forecaster to read, we can add new technology to help us predict severe weather events,for example with the rise of AI we can use it to help us predict if a tornado is on the ground or not and help issue weather warnings faster.
